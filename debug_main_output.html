Courses by Subject


Enrolment in each course is limited and in some cases the demand exceeds that limit. The University is unable to guarantee registration in any particular course and reserves the right to withdraw course offerings.

Courses are assigned to three Categories and students must take 1.0 course from each Category before graduation. For more information, see Breadth Requirements for Bachelor Degrees

Some courses do not provide prerequisites for senior courses. Some senior courses are restricted to honours students.

Some of the courses described in this section may not be offered in the coming year.
